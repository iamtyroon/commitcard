How your credentials are handled

Your token is kept in this tab only (memory + sessionStorage) and sent nowhere except api.github.com. It is never written to disk by this page and disappears when you close the tab. Prefer the CLI for long-lived access: gh auth login.

Signed in through the OAuth broker. Your token stays server-side in an httpOnly cookie — this page never reads it. Revoking the app on GitHub ends the session.
